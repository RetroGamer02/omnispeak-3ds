----------=Keen Asylum=----------
Keen Asylum is a custom level pack for Keen 5 v1.4.  It's main purpose (as it doesn't have a 
storyline or main theme) is to show off some cool TED5/Keen5 exploits and tricks.  While some portions 
of levels might be mentally challenging or test your skills/patience, keep in mind that 
the use of TED5 and in-game developer modes is not only welcomed, but encouraged by the Keen 
Asylum creators.  

A couple of the levels require a fair sized chunk of memory.  When testing them in Windows XP Pro, 
it was unable to play the music at times. The number of on-screen sprites may be limited at times 
as well. In full DOS mode or in a completely legacy compliant environment you should have no 
problem running Keen Asylum.

the zip file should contain:
CK5PATCH.EXE
CUSTMAPS.CK5
GO5.BAT
MAPHEAD.CK5
PATCH5.PAT
UNLZEXE.EXE


To play Keen Asylum, unzip 'Keen Asylum.zip'to a specific place, and copy 
the following Keen 5 (VERSION 1.4 ONLY) files to that directory
KEEN5E.EXE
GAMEMAPS.CK5
AUDIO.CK5
EGAGRAPH.CK5

Unzip Keen5e.exe by
1. Dragging it over UNLZEXE.EXE or
2. opening a command prompt and typing:  UNLZEXE KEEN5E.EXE

Play the game by running 'GO5.BAT'

Enjoy!
