This is The CK Guy's Keen 5.5, ported to Omnispeak.

To run, run path-to-omnispeak/omnispeak /episode ck55.ck5

(Your version of Omnispeak will need the Keen 5 data installed.)

Otherwise, take a copy of Omnispeak (with the Keen 5 data), and copy
this over it, replacing files, then run:
	omnispeak /episode ck55.ck5
